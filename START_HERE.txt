Jet Set Radio Future recomp

Full instructions, for first-time users too: README.md (open it with Notepad,
or read it on the GitHub page).

Short version, in jsrf_recomp\:
  1. copy your game files (default.xbe and the rest) into game\
  2. generate_code.bat     translate your default.xbe into C (Python 3 + capstone)
  3. rebuild.bat           build (Visual Studio 2022 with C++)
  4. build_portable.bat    put jsrf_recomp.exe + jsrf.ini in the game folder,
                           then play by double-clicking jsrf_recomp.exe there
     (or play_jsrf.bat to play from the project; run_jsrf.bat to make a log)
